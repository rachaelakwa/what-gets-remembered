Accession note

In October 2026, I visited the Internet Archive and recorded observations from a tour of its collections, infrastructure, and preservation work.

This site turns a small number of those observations into records. It is not a complete account of the Internet Archive. It is a selective reconstruction of one visit, shaped by what was recorded, what was chosen for inclusion, and how the material is organized here.

You can enter through search, browse by material or question, open a random record, or follow links between related records. Different paths may make different parts of the collection easier to notice.

About this archive

One visit, eight records.

This site was assembled from notes taken during a single visit to the Internet Archive. I chose which moments to turn into records and which material to leave out.

The collection is intentionally small. Its purpose is not to explain the Internet Archive in full, but to preserve a few encounters that can be examined from different directions.
